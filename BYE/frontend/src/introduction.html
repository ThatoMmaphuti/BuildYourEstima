Let's Get to Know You 💜

Before we begin your journey, we'd love to know a little about you. This is your space. Take your time and be yourself.

01. A Little About You

Everyone has a story. Let's start by getting to know the person behind it.

🔒 Your personal information should be kept private. Only share information you feel comfortable providing. You do not need to give us anyone else's contact details.

02. How Are You Feeling Right Now?

Feelings can be complicated. Sometimes we feel more than one emotion at a time. Choose all the emotions that describe how you're feeling today.

You can select more than one emotion. There are no right or wrong answers.

03. Your Story Matters

Now that we've gotten to know you a little, we'd like to understand what brought you here.

You can start by thinking about:
  • What has been happening in your life?
  • What has been bothering you?
  • How long have you been experiencing this?
  • How has it affected your everyday life?
  • What would you like to change?
💜 This is your story. You are not required to share anything you're uncomfortable discussing. Avoid including passwords, addresses or other identifying information. You can also choose to talk about it with a Listener instead.
